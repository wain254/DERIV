Getting started

1. Get your credentials

Create an app at Deriv's API dashboard to get an App ID, and generate an API token with trade scopes.

api.deriv.com →
2. Connect

Paste your App ID and Token in the top bar and click Connect. The dot turns green once authorized.

3. Build a bot

Go to Bot Builder, pick a market and trade type, set your purchase condition and money management, then click Run.

4. Study the market

Use Analysis to inspect digit distribution before choosing a trade type, and AI Scanner for statistical signal summaries.

Synthetic indices are generated by an audited random number generator. These signals are descriptive statistics on recent ticks only — they are not predictions and do not provide a trading edge. Past digit frequency does not affect future ticks. Trade only what you can afford to lose.
Connect and click Rescan to generate a statistical summary.
